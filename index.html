OUR MANIFESTO

The Three Principles

We believe software should solve real bottlenecks without corporate surveillance, forced account sign-ups, or per-seat pricing traps.

01 / LOCAL PRIVACY

Local Processing by Default

If a computation can run inside your browser, it should never touch an external server. Your documents, drafts, and media stay sandboxed on your device with zero cloud inspection.

02 / ZERO FRICTION

Zero-Login Collaboration

We refuse to force your clients, reviewers, or listeners to register accounts, remember passwords, or download apps just to view, comment, or approve your creative work.

03 / FAIR ECONOMICS

Anti-Per-Seat Pricing

No $25/user/month penalties for having more team members or clients. We build tools with flat, transparent pricing and keep our core superpowers free forever.
